Every system I've truly understood, I understood because I broke it first. Not by accident — on purpose.

## The first time

The first time I took apart a network stack, I had no idea what I was doing. I changed one setting, and everything stopped.

## Why breaking works

- You see what depends on what
- You learn the real failure modes, not the documented ones
- You develop intuition that documentation can't give you

> If you can't break it, you don't understand it yet.

That's it. That's the whole method.
